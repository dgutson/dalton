How colourful a bright spot must be to count as an indicator. An LED emits its own narrow band of colour, so it clips coloured; a reflection of a lamp or a window clips white. Strict demands a strong colour and misses the faintest LEDs; loose accepts weaker ones and picks up more reflections with them. Normal was measured best on a real router panel.

Walks every exposure lever the camera claims to have, one at a time, and records what the sensor actually did with each. Two of them have already turned out to be fiction on this phone, so nothing new gets built on a lever until it has been measured. Saves like a scan; needs serve.sh.

Lower exposure if the LED core keeps blowing out to white. Locking white balance stops the camera from cancelling a strong colour cast. The flash is the bluntest of the three and usually the one that works: it lights the room, so the camera stops itself down.
