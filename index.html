Point the circle at one LED, or press SCAN to name every LED in the picture.

Settings

Sample circle

Size

Small = one LED. Large = diffuse or distant sources. Live mode only.

Scan

Find lights that are

How much brighter than the brightest point a thing must be to count as a light. Strict finds only the obvious ones; loose also picks up dim or distant ones — and more false alarms, which is the safer way round. Raised automatically if a bright background would otherwise swallow everything.

Bracket exposure

Takes two extra darker shots and reads each light from whichever one blew out least. Slower (about a second) but it is the main defence against a clipped core. Needs camera exposure control.

Camera

Exposure
—
Lock white balance

Lower exposure if the LED core keeps blowing out to white. Locking white balance stops the camera from cancelling a strong colour cast.

Hue boundaries (°)

Point at a known LED, read the measured hue in the stats line, then nudge the neighbouring boundary until it names it correctly. Saved on this phone.

Dalton

Names the colour of an LED from the camera. Works offline; nothing leaves the phone.
